# Life Plan v2: integrated, rich, and honest about what the outside world offers

Status: proposed, replaces the direction of v1. Decisions D1 to D4 block the phases they name.
Date: 2026-09-03
Supersedes: `2026-09-03-life-plan.md` (kept as the record of what was built and why)

## Why a v2

v1 optimised for privacy and minimum: files instead of OAuth, regexes before a
model, text instead of pictures, a list instead of a timeline. It was internally
consistent and it was not the product Edd asked for. The ask, restated in his
words and taken at face value:

1. **+18 means +18.** Once somebody has affirmed it, photographs and real
   descriptions, and a way to search positions by what the person wants.
2. **Calendars means integration.** Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, working with
   that data live. Not a file somebody exports by hand.
3. **A model that enriches the day.** MiniMax or DeepSeek reading the day and
   suggesting what to do with it, including events near where the person lives.
4. **The design has to be professional.** The current `/day` is a form with a
   list on top.

This document does not argue with any of that. It says what each one costs and
where the outside world refuses, so the decisions are made on facts.

### The one that changes the business, said plainly

**Photographs of real people in sexual positions are "adult content" under
Stripe's restricted-business terms.** Tasteful illustrations with plain
descriptions are wellness education and are generally fine; photographs are not,
and the difference is not ours to argue after the fact. If D1 is "photographs",
enForma cannot bill through Stripe at all — not on a separate account, not with
a policy letter — and moves to an adult-friendly processor (CCBill, Segpay,
Epoch), with their fees (typically 10–15% against Stripe's ~3%), their
onboarding, and a separate hosting review for the images themselves. That is a
different company to run, and it is still a real option. It is just not a
detail.

Illustrations sit on the right side of that line and still need Task 0.2's
written confirmation because of the shared account.

## The four decisions

**D1 — What "+18" means, exactly.**
- (a) Commissioned or licensed **illustrations**, explicit in description,
  anatomical in drawing. Stripe-compatible. One-off cost per set.
- (b) **Photographs.** Not Stripe. New processor, new hosting terms, and every
  image is a model-release question.
- (c) **AI-generated illustrations.** Every mainstream image API forbids sexual
  content in its terms; a few allow non-explicit anatomical art on request. Has
  to be checked vendor by vendor before it is a plan. Cheapest if it works.

Recommendation: (a). It is what the competitors did, and it is the only one
that keeps the processor.

**D2 — Which calendars, and who holds the token.**
Google via OAuth (Edd runs the verification), Apple via CalDAV app-specific
password, Microsoft via Graph. All three mean **our server holds a credential
that can read somebody's whole calendar**, encrypted at rest with a key the
server holds — v1 refused exactly this and v2 reverses it. Recommendation:
Google first, the other two behind it on the same `calendar_links` table.

**D3 — Which events source.**
Ticketmaster free to start, PredictHQ when the ticketed subset is not enough.
Both need `users.area` to become a real place (lat/long, from a geocoder — the
browser's own position with permission, or a city typed and geocoded once).
Recommendation: Ticketmaster + browser geolocation.

**D4 — Design direction.**
Whether `/day` keeps the app's "Instrument" theme (dark chalk, cobalt signal,
Geist) with the structure and motion from the loaded skill applied on top, or
whether Pro surfaces get their own look. Recommendation: keep the palette, take
the structure — one product, not two. The skill's white-on-`#f9fafb` bento is
its default, not a rule, and the app's own `DESIGN.md` wins on colour.

## Revised phases

Everything below reuses the v1 engine where it still holds: the entitlement
(`pro_until`, admin rule, grace window), `buildDay`'s placement arithmetic, the
`.ics` parser (it becomes the CalDAV/Google normaliser rather than the front
door), the intake parser as the offline fallback, the gym-events rule.

### V2.1 — `/day` redesigned (needs D4; nothing external)

The screen is the **day**, not forms. Applying the loaded skill at variance 8,
motion 6, density 4:

- **A real timeline.** Vertical, hours as a ruler, every block a span whose
  height is its duration. Free time is visible *space*, not a row that says
  "2h free". The v1 list was a taste call made for phones; it reads as a
  spreadsheet and the annotation circled exactly that.
- **Now.** A live marker on the ruler, and a "dynamic island" pill at the top
  with what is next and how long until it. Perpetual, sprung.
- **Forms leave the main view.** Anchors, waking window, preferences, calendar
  and intake move into a side sheet (`/day` stays the destination; the sheet
  is `?edit`). The main view has one action: the day.
- **Motion.** Blocks stagger in on load; `layout` transitions when an anchor
  changes so a block visibly slides rather than the list re-rendering;
  spring physics throughout; reduced-motion respected.
- **Materiality.** Inner 1px borders, tinted shadows, the app's own `#141412`
  and cobalt. Mono for every number, as `DESIGN.md` already demands.
- **States.** A composed empty day, skeleton while events load, inline error
  when a calendar token has expired.

`motion` v13 is installed; nothing new is added.

### V2.2 — Calendars, live (needs D2)

- `calendar_links` collection: `owner`, `provider` (`google` | `apple` |
  `microsoft`), encrypted credential, `cursor`, `last_synced`. Readable by the
  owner only, credential never returned by the API.
- Google: OAuth code flow on the server (`/api/enforma/calendar/google/start`,
  `/callback`), `calendar.events.readonly` only, refresh handled server-side.
  Push notifications via Google's watch channels so a change shows up without
  polling.
- Apple: a form for the Apple ID and the app-specific password, verified
  immediately with one `PROPFIND`; then `sync-collection` polling on a
  schedule.
- Microsoft: Graph, same shape as Google.
- Events land as `BusyBlock` rows exactly as the `.ics` importer produces them
  today, so `buildDay` needs no change. Titles are stored (this is the user's
  own calendar on a server they chose; v1's "show, don't store" was for a file
  that might be somebody else's).
- **Write-back**: the session, the plate and the day's blocks appear in *their*
  calendar as events we own, updated when the day changes. This is what
  "trabajar con esos datos" means and it is the half a file can never do.
- Boundary audit from the receiving side: one account cannot see another's
  link, the credential is never in any response, a revoked link stops the
  poll.

### V2.3 — The model reads the day (needs nothing; DeepSeek is env)

- One route, `/api/enforma/day/enrich`, Pro-gated by the same cap the coach
  has. Input: the day's blocks, the free gaps, the person's stated preferences,
  the nearby events from V2.4, the weather if we fetch it. Output, validated to
  a closed shape as `validateBlocks` does: a two-sentence read of the day, one
  suggestion per free gap, and which nearby events are worth the gap they fit.
- Rendered inside the free space on the timeline, which is where the
  suggestion belongs.
- `COACH_BASE_URL=https://api.deepseek.com/v1`, `COACH_MODEL=deepseek-chat`.
  The proxy already substitutes the model and hides the key.
- The privacy sentence above the intake stays: the day goes to a vendor, and
  `coachDestination()` already knows which.

### V2.4 — Events near you (needs D3)

- `users.area` gains `lat`/`lng`, set from the browser's geolocation with
  permission or from a typed place geocoded once (Nominatim, no key).
- `/api/enforma/events/near`: Ticketmaster Discovery, `geoPoint` + `radius`,
  the next 14 days, cached in `shared_cache` per geohash cell for six hours so
  a city of members costs one call rather than one each. 5,000/day is the
  budget and the cache is what makes it hold.
- Shown as a strip below the timeline and fed to V2.3. A tap RSVPs into the
  day as a commitment — the same `event` slot the gym bus already produces.
- PredictHQ behind the same route when Edd decides the ticketed subset is not
  enough; it is a key and a mapping.

### V2.5 — Intimate activity, +18 properly (needs D1 and Task 0.2)

- The library grows from eight entries to a real one: illustrations per D1,
  explicit descriptions, and **search by intent** — effort, who leads, what
  it is kind to, what it is for. Search is local over the library's own
  fields; there is no external API to call and the search will find nothing.
- A model may describe and recommend from the library (`validateProposal`
  shape: ids only, never invented entries).
- The day slot gains a suggestion from the library when the module is on,
  chosen from what the person has said they are working around.
- **What still does not exist:** a log. The DPIA still governs recording
  anybody's sexual activity, and nothing in this phase needs one.
- If D1 is (b), this phase also carries: new processor integration, image
  hosting review, model releases per image, and an age-verification step
  stronger than an affirmation, because a processor in that category will
  require one.

## What survives from v1 unchanged

`pro_until` and the admin rule · the grace window · `buildDay`'s arithmetic ·
the anchor parser as offline fallback · the gym-events rule · the `.ics` parser
as the normaliser behind every calendar provider · every boundary audit.

## What v1 built that v2 removes

The `.ics` file picker as the *front door* to calendars (it stays as an import
of last resort) · the list timeline · the forms on the main `/day` view · the
eight-entry text-only library.
